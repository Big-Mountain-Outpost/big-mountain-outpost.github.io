Delete Your DateDots Account & Data

Last updated: [RELEASE DATE]

DateDots is operated by Big Mountain Outpost, LLC. This page explains how to delete your DateDots account and the data associated with it, whether you signed in with Google or with Apple. App name: DateDots (com.bigmountainoutpost.datedots).

Uninstalling DateDots does not delete your account or your cloud data. Use one of the options below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

This works the same whether you signed in with Google or with Apple:

  1. Open DateDots and sign in, if you are not already.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap "Delete account & data" and confirm.

Before anything is deleted, DateDots may ask you to confirm your sign-in once more. If you cancel that step, nothing is deleted.

The deletion then permanently removes your cloud data and your sign-in account, clears DateDots' data on the device you are using, and cancels the alarms it had set there. It also ends DateDots' access to the account you signed in with:

  • Google: DateDots' access to your Google account is revoked.
  • Apple, on an iPhone or iPad: DateDots asks Apple to revoke its access.
  • Apple, on Android: DateDots cannot ask Apple from an Android device, and tells you so. Your DateDots account and data are still deleted. To also remove DateDots from your Apple ID, on an Apple device go to Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, choose DateDots, and stop using it.

If you have used DateDots on other phones or tablets, the alarms stored on those devices stay there until you delete DateDots or clear its data on each one.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email us from the email address associated with your DateDots account:

bigmountainoutpost@gmail.com

We verify the request by confirming you control that address, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, the address on your account is one Apple created, and we may not be able to reply to it. In that case we will ask you to delete your account in the app while signed in (Option 1), and we may also ask you to tell us the account ID shown in Settings → Account. You can tap it there to copy it.

What gets deleted

  • Your cloud-stored alarms, routines, timeline templates, assigned dates, silenced days, and settings.
  • The records we keep to sync your devices: a random identifier for each device you signed in on, and the list of items you had deleted.
  • Your record of accepting our Terms and Disclaimer.
  • Your sign-in account: your account identifier, and the name, email address and (for Google) profile photo link held for sign-in.
  • Your DateDots Pro entitlement record, if you have one.
  • When you delete in the app: DateDots' data on that device, and the alarms it had set there.

What we keep

  • One purchase record, if you bought DateDots Pro: a one-way hash of your purchase token, linked to your account identifier. It prevents a single purchase from unlocking unlimited accounts, and it contains no other information about you.

We do not retain backups of deleted account data beyond the period required to complete the deletion. See our Privacy Policy for details.
